## How to read
Firms common to our OHLCV files and the HF 10-K corpus, mapped to CIK; S&P-500 members first, then a seeded sector-stratified fill of non-members; every firm carries sp_bucket (SP500 / NonSP500). SURVIVOR-ONLY: the OHLCV set holds current listings only, so firms that delisted before 2026 are absent (survivorship-biased).
